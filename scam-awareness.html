Scam awareness

Warning signs to take seriously

No single sign proves an offer is fraudulent. Use multiple checks and ask for clear, verifiable information.

Urgent payment requests

Be cautious when someone pressures you to transfer money quickly, especially to a personal account or an unverified payment channel.

Unverifiable offer letters

Check the employer, contact details, role, salary, location and document consistency before relying on an offer.

Employer identity is unclear

Ask for the legal employer name and independently verify the organisation and the person claiming to represent it.

Guaranteed visa or job promises

Visa and employment outcomes depend on the relevant authorities and employers. Be cautious of guaranteed approvals or guaranteed jobs.

Pressure to use unknown links

Do not share passwords, OTPs or sensitive account credentials through unsolicited links or messages.
