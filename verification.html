Verification workflow

Four practical steps

This page explains the workflow; use the existing Avenix contact route for a verification enquiry.

1. Collect

Keep the offer letter, contract, employer details, visa-related documents or other relevant information together.

2. Review

Check names, dates, role, salary, location, contact details, formatting and consistency across the documents.

3. Verify

Where appropriate, independently check the employer, agency or other claimed source rather than relying only on contact details in the document.

4. Decide

Use the available evidence to decide your next step. Verification is not a guarantee of a visa, job or outcome.
