Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about work opportunities, study pathways, verification and enquiries.

How do I know whether a job is currently hiring?

On the Work Opportunity page, a published listing marked “Live Hiring” is currently shown as open for enquiries. A published listing marked “Hiring Closed” remains available for reference but is not shown as currently open.

What information should I check in a job post?

Check the country, city, position, salary, employment type, number of positions, interview requirement, experience, requirements, benefits and application information where provided.

Does Avenix guarantee a job or visa?

No website guidance can guarantee an employer decision, admission, visa approval or immigration outcome. These decisions depend on employers, institutions and relevant authorities.

Can I share a job with someone?

Yes. Open the job details and use Share opportunity or Copy link. The link can be sent through your preferred messaging platform.

What documents may be required?

Requirements vary by role, institution, destination and pathway. Always use the specific requirements stated for the opportunity and confirm the current requirements before submitting documents.

Can Lara answer questions in my language?

Lara is designed to respond in the language and style you use, including English, Hindi, Hinglish, Urdu and several other languages.

How can I contact Avenix?

Use the existing Avenix consultation form, phone number or WhatsApp contact shown throughout the website.
